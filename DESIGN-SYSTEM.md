# Carryover Design System

## Point of view

Carryover is an industrial logbook expressed as a control room: direct, dense, traceable, and calm under pressure. It does not use Meridian’s editorial photography, serif typography, or cinematic motion.

## Foundations

- Graphite surfaces identify persistent navigation and protected operating decisions.
- Steel gray rules create structure without decorative cards.
- Safety amber marks active work and required attention; severity always includes text.
- Inter carries operating copy; IBM Plex Mono carries IDs, time, state, and audit metadata.
- Desktop supports comparison; tablet pairs related tasks; mobile becomes a direct action sequence.

## Components and states

- Workspace navigation: active route, visible role preview, local reset.
- Issue records: severity, status, equipment, owner, due date, decision, evidence, immutable audit.
- Forms: visible labels, limits, validation copy, disabled permission state.
- Reports: filters, printable brief, CSV export, disclosure.
- Notices: recovery and local-save status use live regions.

## Accessibility and motion

Controls meet a 44px interaction target. Focus is visible. Native form controls preserve keyboard behavior. Mobile tables become document-order records. Motion is limited to the navigation drawer and is disabled under reduced motion. Color never carries status alone.

## Content voice

Short, operational, and specific. Copy distinguishes observations, decisions, actions, approvals, and evidence. Fictional-data and demonstration-permission boundaries are always explicit.
